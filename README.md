## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an **AI Product Engineer with 3+ years of software engineering experience** across applied AI, full-stack development, backend systems, and blockchain infrastructure.

Currently, I build and operate AI-powered products used by **1,500+ free users and 100+ paid customers**. My work spans the complete product lifecycle:

- Understanding ambiguous product and customer requirements
- Rapidly building and validating prototypes
- Developing production frontend and backend systems
- Integrating and orchestrating multiple AI models
- Building retrieval, crawling, and asynchronous processing pipelines
- Deploying and maintaining production services

I enjoy working on problems where AI must do more than generate text—systems that need to retrieve information, select the right model, execute multi-stage workflows, process long-running jobs, and produce reliable user-facing results.

My previous experience includes delivering **30+ production smart contracts** and supporting services across multiple EVM networks.

## 💡 How I Work

I am most effective when working on open-ended product problems that require both engineering depth and product judgment.

My typical workflow:

1. Understand the user problem and business requirements
2. Identify the smallest useful prototype
3. Build and validate the complete system
4. Design the production architecture
5. Add reliability, retries, caching, observability, and testing
6. Deploy, collect feedback, and iterate

I care about:

- Product ownership
- Direct communication with users and stakeholders
- Reliable production systems
- Clear technical trade-offs
- Fast iteration without sacrificing quality
- Building features that create measurable user value
